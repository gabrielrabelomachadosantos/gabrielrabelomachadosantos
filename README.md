## Ludolinx

Ludolinx is the product I am building for the gaming world: a social network where players can discover games, follow communities, create posts, share gaming moments, manage favorite games, and explore content around releases and recommendations.

The platform combines a public-facing Angular experience with backend services for users, games, engagement, store/subscriptions, payments, notifications, media, and creator flows. The ecosystem includes features like LudoBot, video generation credits, subscription plans, saved payment methods, and game discovery across a large catalog.
